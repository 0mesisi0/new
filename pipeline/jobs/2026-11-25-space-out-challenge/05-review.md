# Review 2026-11-25-space-out-challenge

## Blocking

1. **The notification covers the timer at 320 px.** In the browser preview, the settled notification spans y=10–74 and the timer spans y=34–77. The notification sits above the timer and obscures the score while the player is trying to stay still. Give the notification and timer separate space, including when the notification wraps. [index.html](C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/space-out-challenge/index.html:19)
2. **The results overlay blocks the sound button.** The button sits at `z-index: 6` under the full-screen results overlay at `z-index: 40`, so mouse and touch users cannot reach it. Move it into the results layer or above the overlay. Apply the 600 ms input guard to the sound action too. `M` currently changes sound during that guard. [index.html](C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/space-out-challenge/index.html:30) · [input guard](C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/space-out-challenge/index.html:298)
3. **Game controls miss native button activation.** Start, retry, copy, and sound respond to `pointerdown` and custom key handlers, so assistive technology that activates a button through `click` does nothing. A right-click on Start also begins the countdown. Use native `click` activation for these controls and keep immediate pointer failure during play. The `touchstart` fallback should also identify trap targets so it reports the correct reason. [index.html](C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/space-out-challenge/index.html:305)
4. **Resizing can end a run on movement within the 10 px tolerance.** The resize handler clears the mouse anchor. If no move arrives during the 200 ms resize grace period, the first move after 300 ms fails regardless of distance. The event-handler check reproduced this with a 1 px move on each axis. Preserve the existing anchor and let resize-grace events update it when they occur. [resize handler](C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/space-out-challenge/index.html:166) · [movement handler](C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/space-out-challenge/index.html:318)
5. **The held-touch warning becomes hard to read, and zoom is disabled on every screen.** At the dimmest point of the blink, the warning is about 1.4:1 against the room color, below the 3:1 threshold for large text. Keep the text legible throughout the warning. Allow zoom on the instructions and results screens while suppressing game gestures during play. [viewport](C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/space-out-challenge/index.html:5) · [warning styles](C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/space-out-challenge/index.html:31)

Hide the decorative glyph from assistive technology or label the card as a single action.
