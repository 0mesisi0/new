# Review 2026-11-25-crank-carol-music-box

## Blocking

1. **Demo mode can overwrite the lifetime turn counter.** `#demo` resets the in-memory counter to two turns, then the shared `pagehide` handler saves it. A runtime check confirmed that closing the demo replaced a saved count of 57 with 2. Demo controls can also change settings and the thumbnail state. Disable input and persistence in demo mode. [Demo initialization](C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/crank-carol-music-box/index.html:669)
2. **Touch painting and grid targets fall short at phone widths.** The compose grid fits six column widths at 320 px and seven at 390 px before scrollbar space. At the initial position, the clickable edge cells are only about 9–10 px and 36–39 px wide. In the vertically overflowing grid, painting starts only with a horizontal gesture, so a straight vertical stroke cannot paint a column. Fast strokes can also skip intermediate cells. Provide eight columns per strip view with full 44 px targets and a touch interaction that supports continuous painting and vertical scrolling. [Grid sizing](C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/crank-carol-music-box/index.html:417) · [Pointer handling](C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/crank-carol-music-box/index.html:464)
3. **The compose cursor can edit an offscreen cell.** Enter immediately after opening Compose changes the default cell in the middle row. That row starts 308 px down, beyond the visible grid at a 480 px viewport height. The highlight appears only after the edit. Show the cursor and scroll it into view when the grid receives focus. [Compose entry](C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/crank-carol-music-box/index.html:262) · [Keyboard editing](C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/crank-carol-music-box/index.html:342)
4. **Keyboard focus is lost on transitions.** Closing the code panel with Escape leaves focus on the document body. Starting the box and leaving Compose can also hide the focused control without assigning a new target. Move focus to the selected song chip after Start, back to the Load code button when its panel closes, and back to Make my tune after Done or Escape. [Panel focus](C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/crank-carol-music-box/index.html:287) · [Start](C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/crank-carol-music-box/index.html:321)
5. **Fast pointer movement bypasses the playback speed cap.** The drag handler caps the velocity used by the flywheel, then advances playback by up to two steps per pointer event regardless of elapsed time. A stream of 60 maximum-size clockwise events per second can advance 120 steps per second against the specified 40-step cap. Apply the time-based cap to direct drag advancement too. [Crank advance](C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/crank-carol-music-box/index.html:447)
6. **Several grid labels and the progress indicator have low contrast.** The amber and green note labels measure about 3.6:1 against the paper at 13 px. The green progress fill is about 1.4:1 against its track. Darken the affected labels and lighten the progress fill so its position is easier to see. [Note labels](C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/crank-carol-music-box/index.html:631) · [Progress styling](C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/crank-carol-music-box/index.html:25)
