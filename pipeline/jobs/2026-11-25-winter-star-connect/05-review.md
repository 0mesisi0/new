# Review 2026-11-25-winter-star-connect

## Blocking

1. **A cancelled drag can draw a line on mobile.** At 320 × 480, releasing 13 px from Alnitak and 16 px from Alnilam draws the line to Alnilam even though Alnitak is the closest star. The release lookup excludes the starting star before choosing a target. Resolve the nearest target first, then cancel if it is the starting star. Also track whether the pointer crossed the drag threshold during the gesture so a drag that returns to its starting point does not become a tap. [index.html](/C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/winter-star-connect/index.html:349)
2. **Tab skips the stars.** Every star button has `tabIndex = -1`, and the game has no Tab handler for star navigation. Tab moves to the next page control while the canvas star focus stays where it was. Implement the specified Tab and Shift+Tab navigation with a way to leave the board for Hint and Sound. [index.html](/C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/winter-star-connect/index.html:379)
3. **The viewport disables zoom on browsers that honor it.** This makes the small fact and result text harder to read for low-vision players. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `user-scalable=no`. Keep the touch handling scoped to the game board. [index.html](/C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/winter-star-connect/index.html:5)
4. **The score timer can count intro time and lose visible play time.** The frame that ends the intro adds its full interval to the score. A visible frame gap longer than two seconds is also truncated, which can affect the best-record tiebreaker. Measure visible time at the play and visibility transitions, and keep the 50 ms cap on animation updates.
