# Review 2026-11-25-christmas-long-weekend

## Blocking

1. **Returning the same piece twice can break the game loop.** Pick up a piece again while it is flying back to the tray, then drop it outside the board again. The same piece enters `flies` twice. When one entry clears `p.fly`, the other reads `p.fly.t` and throws. This reproduced in Chromium. Keep one return animation per piece and cancel it when the piece is picked up. [Drag return](/C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/christmas-long-weekend/index.html:390) · [Animation update](/C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/christmas-long-weekend/index.html:459)
2. **Later boards clip tray pieces on mobile.** At 320 × 480, the tray extended past the canvas in 49 of 100 seeded six-row boards tested, by up to 35 px. Clipping also reproduced at 390 × 480 and 390 × 844. The layout can shrink grid cells to 36 px against the 52 px minimum, and clipping cuts into the nominal 44 px hit areas. Use the same tray layout to measure and draw, and fit the entire tray within the playable area. [Layout sizing](/C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/christmas-long-weekend/index.html:250)
3. **Drops can use a previous pointer position.** `pointerup` uses the last animation frame’s snap. A quick release can reject a valid drop or place the piece in the previously highlighted cell. Recompute and validate the snap from the release coordinates before committing. A stationary tap on a placed piece also does nothing, despite the specified tap-to-rotate behavior. [Pointer handlers](/C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/christmas-long-weekend/index.html:378) · [Snap calculation](/C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/christmas-long-weekend/index.html:454)
4. **Keyboard focus and activation break across screens.** On game over, Enter and Space are intercepted even when **Copy result** or the sound toggle has focus. The play controls also remain in the tab order behind the results overlay, and several handlers explicitly blur the activated button. Preserve native button activation, move focus on screen changes, and make the covered controls inert. [Game-over transition](/C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/christmas-long-weekend/index.html:316) · [Keyboard handler](/C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/christmas-long-weekend/index.html:407)
5. **Screen readers cannot follow the puzzle.** The canvas provides no accessible representation of the tray pieces, board occupancy, or placement preview. A screen-reader user can press the number and arrow keys without knowing what is selected, where it is, or whether it fits. Expose those states and announce placement feedback. [Play markup](/C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/christmas-long-weekend/index.html:83) · [Canvas preview](/C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/christmas-long-weekend/index.html:542)
6. **Small text falls below 4.5:1 contrast.** White on the calendar’s `#e0453d` red is 4.13:1. Red and blue weekday text on `#fdf8ef` measure 3.90:1 and 4.46:1, respectively. Adjust the small-text colors while retaining the weekday distinctions. [Calendar colors](/C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/christmas-long-weekend/index.html:39) · [Board headers](/C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/christmas-long-weekend/index.html:502)
7. **Reduced motion still includes a failed-drop shake.** The canvas return animation always applies a sine-wave wobble, and piece pops and floating bonus text also keep moving. Disable the shake and make the remaining feedback static when reduced motion is requested. [Return and pop effects](/C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/christmas-long-weekend/index.html:528) · [Bonus animation](/C:/work/haruhanpan/play/items/christmas-long-weekend/index.html:553)
8. **The bilingual UI omits required copy.** Korean mode still shows `DEC 2026`, `FRI`, `SAT`, and `SUN` in the start calendar. The results screen never renders the localized score label and hides the completed-weekend count when it is zero. Localize the calendar and render both results fields.
